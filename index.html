🧑‍🎓 Origin of Peter Parker

Peter Parker is an ordinary high school student living in New York with his Uncle Ben and Aunt May. One day, during a science experiment, he gets bitten by a radioactive spider. This bite gives him incredible powers:

  • Super strength
  • Wall-crawling ability
  • Spider-sense (danger detection)
  • Enhanced agility

At first, Peter uses his powers selfishly—to earn money and fame.

💔 The Turning Point: Uncle Ben

Peter lets a criminal escape because he doesn’t think it’s his problem. Later, that same criminal kills Uncle Ben.

👉 “With great power comes great responsibility.”

From that moment, Peter decides to use his powers to help others and becomes Spider-Man.

🦸‍♂️ Life as Spider-Man

Peter struggles to balance:

  • School and later college
  • Relationships (like with Mary Jane Watson and Gwen Stacy)
  • Financial problems
  • His secret identity

Meanwhile, he fights famous villains like the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, and Venom. He is often misunderstood and criticized by the public, especially by the Daily Bugle newspaper.

🕸️ What Makes It Special?

  • Focuses on real-life struggles along with superhero action
  • Peter is relatable—he faces failure, guilt, and responsibility
  • His stories are about growing up, sacrifice, and doing the right thing

🎬 In Short

Spider-Man’s story isn’t just about powers—it’s about learning responsibility, dealing with loss, and choosing to be a hero even when life is hard.
